About the Summit

The summit exists to reshape how students see agriculture, expose them to opportunities in agricultural innovation and agribusiness, and inspire a new generation of innovators, entrepreneurs and change-makers. Agriculture is more than farming. It is an ecosystem of innovation, enterprise and opportunity.

Vision

To inspire innovative, entrepreneurial and opportunity-driven young people who see agriculture as a pathway to economic empowerment and sustainable growth.

Mission

To expose students to agribusiness and agricultural innovation opportunities, promote innovative thinking toward food security, and position agriculture as a sector for entrepreneurship and economic transformation.

Objectives

  • Expand awareness beyond traditional farming
  • Promote youth entrepreneurship across value chains
  • Encourage technology, research and creativity
  • Promote food security and sustainable food systems
  • Transform negative perceptions of agriculture
  • Encourage cross-disciplinary participation
  • Build links with speakers and stakeholders
  • Inspire practical action through ideas and ventures

Three Summit Pillars

01

Agribusiness Opportunities for Youth

Beyond the Farm: Unlocking Agribusiness Opportunities for Youth

Processing, packaging, input supply, commodity trading, logistics, consultancy, business planning, finance and starting small.

Outcome: a broader understanding of agribusiness opportunities.

02

Innovation for Food Security

Innovating for Food Security: The Role of Youth in Transforming Agriculture

Agri-tech, climate-smart agriculture, storage, post-harvest loss, processing, distribution and research.

Outcome: understanding how innovation addresses food-system challenges.

03

Mindset Change: Rethinking Agriculture

Changing the Narrative: Agriculture as an Industry of Opportunity

Challenging misconceptions, modern agriculture, opportunity creation, entrepreneurial thinking, market gaps and careers beyond production.

Outcome: a wider view of agriculture and the student’s place in it.

FAQ

What is the summit?

A student-focused event showing that agriculture is a field of innovation, enterprise and opportunity.

Who can attend?

Any BUK student from SCE, New Site, Old Site or AKTH, in any faculty or level, plus youths interested in agriculture.

Is it free?

Yes. Attendance is free but registration is required.

When and where?

14 November 2026, 9:00 AM prompt, Convocation Arena, Bayero University Kano, Main Campus.

Will I get a certificate?

Every attendee receives a certificate of participation.

Can I get more than one ticket?

No. One meal ticket per profile. Regenerating with the same email shows your original ticket.

How is my data used?

Only for event planning, communication and ticketing. It is not sold or shared with third parties. Photos and video taken at the event may be used for event reporting.
